Scenes

Save the current mode, palette, and tuning as a named scene. Scenes never include recorded audio or microphone access.

    Motion and flashes

    Motion

    Scales the shockwaves, sparkles and brightness jumps from taps, release pulses, the Pulse button and beats. Off turns sudden bursts off entirely. Reduced motion also slows the field to at most 60% speed, stops beats from setting off bursts, caps bursts at 30%, and cuts sparkles and jitter. These controls reduce motion; they aren’t a guarantee of comfort or safety for any condition. Freeze stops all motion. Reset keeps these two settings.

    Live controls

    In the dock: Pulse (P) sends a shockwave from the center. Freeze (F) holds the picture still until you press it again; sound is still analyzed for the meters, and the microphone is still released if the page goes to the background. Intensity scales how strongly sound drives everything and stays put when scenes change. The scene list (or [ and ]) loads saved scenes. A scene in the same mode blends in over 1.5 seconds; one in another mode switches at once. Changing a scene setting by hand clears the selection. The camera button (S) saves the picture as a PNG, without the controls, at the current render resolution. It never includes sound.

    Sound

    Sensitivity sets how strongly sound drives the field. Lower smoothing feels snappier; higher smoothing calms jitter from speech and noisy rooms.

    Bass, midrange and treble set how much each band drives the field, on top of sensitivity; 0% mutes a band. Beats are detected from the bass band. The noise threshold ignores quiet sound, such as room hum, below that share of the recent peak. All four apply to the microphone and the demo signal.

    Motion

    Touch

    How hard dragging fingers push, pull and swirl particles, in every mode. 0% disables drag forces. Geometry twist and pinch remain active, and taps still send shockwaves.

    Release pulseSends a shockwave when you lift your last finger after a drag. A two-finger drag sends a bigger one, with a flash, a second ring and sparks. Scales with touch strength.

    About sound

    PULSAR LIVE listens only through your microphone and analyzes it in real time on this device. It never records, stores, or uploads audio. It can’t capture sound playing inside Apple Music, Spotify, or other apps, so play music out loud nearby instead. If this page is embedded somewhere that blocks the microphone, Demo mode keeps everything running.

    Resetting keeps your saved scenes and your motion settings. It returns the performance profile to Balanced and intensity to 100%.

    PULSAR LIVE needs Canvas 2D, which this browser didn’t provide. Try a current version of Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge.
